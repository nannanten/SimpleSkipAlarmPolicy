Have you ever turned off an alarm because you didn't need it the next day, only to forget to turn it back on afterward?

With Simple Skip Alarm, there's no need to turn off or delete your alarm. Simply skip it when you don't need it.

Once the skipped alarm has passed, your regular schedule stays in place, reducing the chance of forgetting to turn your alarm back on.
